The trip

A week that balances Bali's two souls — the green, spiritual heart around Ubud and the dramatic cliffs of the south. Water temples, a sunrise volcano trek, a day on Nusa Penida and the Kecak fire dance as the sun sets over Uluwatu.

Day by day

Your trip captain meets you at Ngurah Rai Airport with a flower garland and cold coconut. Drive through the countryside to your private pool villa in the jungle. Evening free to wander Ubud market and the Monkey Forest road.

Alaya Ubud Welcome dinner Private transfer

Beat the crowds at Tegallalang with an early start, then a slow coffee tasting at a family plantation. Afternoon purification ritual at Tirta Empul and a golden-hour walk along Campuhan Ridge.

Breakfast Private car & guide

A 2 AM pickup for a guided trek to the summit in time for sunrise over Lake Batur. Breakfast is cooked in volcanic steam. Afternoon soak in natural hot springs, then back to Ubud to rest.

Guided trek Breakfast

Check in to your beachfront resort in Seminyak, with a stop at the Tanah Lot sea temple for sunset. Evening at one of the island's best beach clubs.

The Legian Breakfast

Fast boat across to Nusa Penida for Kelingking Beach, Broken Beach and Angel's Billabong, finishing with snorkelling among manta rays at Crystal Bay.

Fast boat Lunch

A slow morning by the pool. In the afternoon, Uluwatu temple on its 70 m cliff, the Kecak dance at sunset, and a candlelit seafood dinner on Jimbaran beach.

Kecak tickets Seafood dinner

Free time for last-minute shopping or one final swim, then a private transfer to the airport for your flight home.
